# PBUA — POST-BINARY UPGRADE ARCHITECTURE

## Intellectual Property, Conceptual Development & AI-Assisted Engineering Attribution

**PBUA (Post-Binary Upgrade Architecture)** is a conceptual computing, communications, photonic-processing, and hardware/software architecture developed by **Eric C. Lindau** under the Wiz-Dimensional project framework.

The PBUA project encompasses a developing family of related concepts, architectures, system mappings, technical proposals, terminology, diagrams, documentation, interface concepts, signal representations, encoding approaches, photonic and electronic processing concepts, display and adapter concepts, communications architectures, compatibility concepts, and other original project material distributed across the associated PBUA repositories and project documentation.

These materials include, but are not limited to, the conceptual development of **high-radix symbolic data representation, A–Z photonic symbol/channel concepts, wavelength/frequency-domain multiplexing, phase and amplitude modulation concepts, optical and wireless dual-domain communication, EL-40 architecture, PBUA adapters and interfaces, routing concepts, backward/forward compatibility concepts, system-level mappings, and related post-binary computing and communications architectures.**

The PBUA project is intended as a **conceptual research and engineering-development framework**. Individual concepts may be revised, replaced, combined, separated, experimentally tested, simulated, or otherwise developed as the project progresses.
